A simple housekeeping supply checklist

The categories every housekeeping store should cover, plus a few habits that stop shortages before they happen.

Housekeeping 29 September 2026 · 1 min read

Running out of a basic supply in the middle of a busy day slows every room turnaround after it. A short, shared checklist keeps the store stocked and the team moving.

The core categories

Most housekeeping stores need the same groups of products, whatever the size of the property:

  • Surface care: cleaners suited to bathrooms, glass, floors and general surfaces.
  • Hygiene: wet wipes, tissue and hand-care products for rooms and public areas.
  • Guest replenishment: the amenities that get restocked in every room.
  • Tools and consumables: cloths, liners and anything that wears out with use.

Count on a fixed day

Pick one day each week to count stock against a minimum level for each item. When anything falls below its minimum, it goes on the next order. A fixed rhythm is easier to keep than counting “when there’s time.”

Store by use, not by delivery

Keep the items used together close together, and put the fastest-moving products at the front. It saves minutes on every trolley refill, and those minutes add up over a month.

Fewer suppliers, fewer gaps

Every extra supplier is another delivery to track and another invoice to check. Sourcing several categories from one partner makes ordering faster and keeps quality consistent.
